Typing SFX is an NVDA add-on designed to enhance your typing experience through clear and customizable audio feedback. Each keystroke comes alive with distinct sounds, It also supports application-based sound profiles, helping you type with confidence.

Why Choose Typing SFX?

Typing SFX adds a practical and enjoyable audio layer to your workflow. Whether you're aiming for better accuracy or want a more engaging typing experience, this add-on helps make typing smoother, more intuitive, and more satisfying.

Features:

  • Real-Time Typing Sounds: Hear instant sound cues for characters, spaces, backspaces, and Enter. Choose from multiple sound themes that suit your preference and typing rhythm.
  • Whole Word Echo: Speaks the entire word after you finish typing or editing it, instead of announcing only the newly typed characters.
  • Dedicated Number Feedback: Enable precise audio cues for digits so you never miss a number, even while entering complex data.
  • Application-Based Sound Profiles: Create separate sound profiles for individual applications. Manage your profiles from NVDA Menu > Tools > Typing SFX Profiles.
  • Simple Profile Management: Easily create, rename, edit, or remove custom profiles with automatic detection of currently running applications.
  • Custom Text in Protected Fields: Allows you to customize the text or symbol announced for protected fields.
  • Password & Secure Field Control: Type confidently in protected fields by choosing whether numbers are spoken, sounded, or both—balancing privacy and awareness.
  • Simple Settings Panel: Manage all options directly from NVDA's settings, including sound themes, number feedback, and on/off toggles.
